READY % = the weakest gate. Each book is scored on the gate it is furthest from satisfying, so 82% means "its worst gate is 82% of the way there" — not "82% likely to trade". Once a candidate exists the last gate is the score itself, so READY% becomes score ÷ threshold. Gates that are yes/no (COUNTER_TREND, MTF_OPPOSES) cannot be a percentage — they show 0% and say so.
